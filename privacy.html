Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Farlish Studio ("we", "our", or "us") provides bespoke web design, front-end development, conversion optimization, and SEO services for small businesses, contractors, and startups. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of our clients, prospective clients, and website visitors.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our project inquiry form, schedule a discovery call via Calendly, or chat with us on WhatsApp:

  • Contact Details: Your name, work email address, company name, website URL, and phone number.
  • Project Specifications: Target budget, desired page count, timeline preferences, design inspirations, and functional requirements.
  • Technical Data: Browser user agent, approximate geographic location, and IP address for security, anti-spam, and analytics purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Review your project scope and deliver an accurate quote or proposal.
  • Schedule and conduct discovery calls via Google Meet / Calendly.
  • Communicate project milestones, design previews, and sprint deliverables.
  • Maintain client records, deliver invoices, and provide post-launch support.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Client Asset Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Any proprietary business data, trade secrets, unreleased product screenshots, customer lists, or credentials shared with Farlish Studio during the course of a web design sprint are treated with strict confidentiality. We will never share your private project files without your express consent.

5. Third-Party Integrations

Our website uses carefully selected industry-standard tools to facilitate client communication:

  • Calendly: For booking 30-minute discovery calls directly on our calendar.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): For instant quick inquiries.
  • FormSubmit: For encrypted transmission of project briefs to our agency email.

6. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as California or the European Union), you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for complete deletion of your contact records. To exercise these rights, simply email us at hello@farlish.com.
